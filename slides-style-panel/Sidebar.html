The house checklist
  1. Body 14 pt minimum, labels 12 pt (10 in page). No pasted spreadsheet screenshots.
  2. Sentence-case titles. State the conclusion where there is one.
  3. Palette only: navy, azure, orange, light blue, light gray, white. CAD on white.
  4. One accent method per slide.
  5. Images stay inside the content area. The footer stays visible.
  6. End slide says "what's next." Delete trailing blank slides.
  7. Agenda matches the slides. One subtitle and date format. No spaces or "&" in file names.
  8. Supporting math and detail tables go in an appendix after next steps.
