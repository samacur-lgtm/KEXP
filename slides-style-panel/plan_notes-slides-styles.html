Spanner Style Panel: one panel, every deck

A Google Slides sidebar that applies Spanner styles and checks a deck against the house rules from the 2026 slide review. v2 adds the Check tab and is set up to ship once, to the whole domain.

Last updated 2026-10-10PlanNotes

How a deck gets checked

Data flow of the Check tab. The analyzer in the middle is plain code that is tested outside Google. The dashed line is the fix path back into the deck.

Decisions

Seven decisions shape v2. Details and trade-offs are in the notes.

Ship as a private add-on

Decision
One add-on installed for the Spanner domain.
Why
Per-deck paste was the barrier, and nothing updated.
Implication
Needs a Workspace admin, once.

Slides advanced service

Decision
Use the Slides service, not a hand-fetched token.
Why
Fewer scopes, and one call reads the whole deck.
Implication
The manifest must enable it.

Analyze JSON, not objects

Decision
Rules are pure functions on one JSON read.
Why
Fast on big decks and testable without Google.
Implication
Each rule ships with a fixture and a test.

Scale to page size

Decision
Sizes are for the 10 in page and scale by width.
Why
The 14 pt floor was measured on that page.
Implication
Styles match on any page size.

Fix all only when safe

Decision
Auto-fix yellow and double spaces only.
Why
Size, case and deletions can change meaning.
Implication
Other fixes are one click, with a revision guard.

Six-color palette

Decision
Navy, azure, orange, light blue, light gray, white. Green for sustainability only.
Why
The review's palette-only rule.
Implication
Black and mid gray are flagged.

Fix the master once

Decision
"what's next." goes on the End layout, not on slides.
Why
Every reviewed deck was missing it.
Implication
The text box needs one nudge.

What the panel stores, over time

The style data model grew in small steps. Nothing was renamed, so older decks keep working.

  • May 2026: v1Text style (font, size, bold, italic, color, spacing, alignment, indent, bullets) and shape style (fill, border, weight, vertical alignment, autofit), saved per deck.
  • May 2026: brand auditHeadlines to Regular, caption to italic navy, label to azure, azure hex fixed, Success Box replaced by Azure Block.
  • Oct 2026: v2Adds a highlight color to text styles, sizes stored on the 10 in page, five new built-in styles, and the Check findings with their fix data.
  • NextShared styles across decks, if per-deck turns out to be too limiting.
